A working log. The day began after the Prompt House Style was retired and the Implementer-first course was set

## Shipped (commits on master, nothing pushed)

- A hand-written two-step order plan, a hidden acceptance test for it (validated against a reference and four
  mutants), a scorer check for what the model's own tests cover, and plan variants at three detail levels.
- Three more greenfield Java stories with hidden tests (loans, shipping, stock), `--task story:<name>`, and plans per
  story; a prompt-variant switch (`lean`, `persona`), a retry-mode switch (`restart`), a BigDecimal scale hint and a
  `--context-extra` line, all off by default; a retry replay (`retry_replay`) that measures what a retry should show.
- A change log of every prompt, plan and planner change with its evidence and how general it is.

## Findings

1. **The hand plan passes, and the extra system rules are not shown to matter.** The order plan scored 10/10 on the
   full system prompt, the lean one and a persona line alike (all at the ceiling).
2. **Gates are not behaviour.** 30 runs passed every gate while the model's own tests ignored the exception message and
   the boundary; a hidden test over the same runs passed all 49 that completed. The weakness was in the tests, then,
   not the code.
3. **Plan detail decides the result.** One line per step fails on every story (0, 0, 1, 0 of 10); prose with every
   rule passes order (9/10) and stock (10/10) but not loans (0/10) or, by a run, shipping (5/10); a checklist of tests
   with concrete values copied from the feature's example tables passed order (9/10) and loans (10/10) and shipping
   6/10. The example tables do not cover every rule, and the model implements what they show (shipping: a negative
   surcharge for 1 gram).
4. **The first attempt is the limiting step, and retries rarely help.** On the current prompt 22 of 24 early retries,
   and most later ones, repeated the failed answer byte for byte; in a replay a clean restart passed 10 of 28 against
   1 of 28 for the retry as sent. A stated Java fact or a computed hint helped only where the model had merely
   forgotten `setScale` (order) and not where it wrote `BigDecimal.ZERO` in its own test (loans).
5. **Layered, immutable Java is not the hard part.** Stock (record, interface, in-memory repository, service)
   passed 10/10 at prose level; arithmetic with BigDecimal is what fails.

## What went wrong, honestly

- I built a recommendation on the stored runs of 10-08..12 and the maintainer pointed out that they carry far more
  context noise than the current prompts; the numbers were history, not evidence about the current Implementer. I
  corrected the log and the plan (the replay was redone on current-prompt failures).
- The first replay had an off-by-one in the "files to create" parser, so its free-probe shortcut did not work (a minor
  effect) and a second version refused every submission; both found by reading the transcripts, not the summary.
- A first draft of the checklist plan was folded by YAML into one run-on line; found in the first run's transcript.
- Three predictions were falsified outright (restart no better than a failure text, the sides hint, the stated fact on
  loans) and two missed by one run; they are logged as such. One result landed under the wrong entry and was moved.
- Cut for time: the real-harness test of the restart retry (R3), the `current` arms for the new stories and stock at L1.
